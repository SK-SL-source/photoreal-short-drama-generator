# 4 影片提示詞（第 4 站寫提示詞時讀）

提示詞是從 `已核可/3-分鏡/` 的分鏡卡編譯出來的：`{…}` 只能從分鏡卡、資產表、場景關係表抄，欄位以外的字一個都不動；卡上沒寫的，回第 3 站把卡補好，不在提示詞裡補。句型查 `查表-H3句型.md`；每支送件前過 `scripts/h3_prompt_lint.py`，❌ 不送。

**{風格句}**＝`presets/畫風.md` 的 H3 風格句 `Photoreal 3D animation, {光態}, warm neutral low-saturation colors with soft contrast, natural matte skin, normal skin tones, realistic detail.`（只填 {光態}）；後面不再寫任何畫質、皮膚、色調形容。

## 1. 每一鏡用哪個模式

| 這一鏡 | 模式 | 範本 |
|---|---|---|
| 只要聲音：音色檔、旁白、心聲 | 音訊 pass（`comfy.h3_voice`／`comfy.h3_vo`） | — |
| 無台詞動作鏡 | 參考模式：首幀＋角色裁圖＋場景卡（`comfy.h3_ref`，`guide0=True`） | A |
| 對白鏡 | 同上，再掛說話人的音色檔 | B |
| 心聲鏡 | 同 A；全片無人聲、嘴閉、不掛音色檔；心聲另外生、剪接時放 | A |
| 首幀來源是「無」的鏡 | 參考模式只掛裁圖和場景卡，不釘首幀 | C |
| 結尾要停在指定構圖 | A 或 B 再加尾幀（和首幀同機位） | D |
| 一鏡拆兩段 | 每段一支；B 段首幀＝A 段接點那格（佇列 `after`） | A／B |

釘幀：首幀 `guide0=True` 釘第 0 幀；尾幀 `end_frame=…`；首尾都釘靜止姿勢片子幾乎不動，要有動作就只釘首幀。首幀是軟約束，和提示詞的景別、內容衝突時 H3 照文字走，所以起手狀態要寫得和首幀一樣。

## 2. 範本（`{…}` 照分鏡卡填；用不到的句子整句刪）

**A. 無台詞動作鏡：參考模式＋首幀＋角色裁圖＋場景卡**

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is {角色寫法}, {年齡}, on the character sheet <Picture 2>, with {4–6 個看得見的外觀錨點}.
<Subject 2> is {角色寫法}, {年齡}, on the character sheet <Picture 3>, with {…}.
<Subject 3> is {場景} on the scene sheet <Picture 4>, with {地標、具名光源}.
<Picture 1> is the first frame of [Shot 1], showing {誰在哪、兩人距離、正在做什麼}.

summary:
[keyframe completion + reference generation] A {秒}-second vertical 9:16 {景別詞} of {誰} in <Subject 3> that begins from <Picture 1>. {主動作和同一瞬間的後果}. {收尾狀態}.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - {臉、髮、服裝} from <Picture 2>.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - {臉、髮、服裝} from <Picture 3>{, and 道具 from <Picture 1>}.
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - {地標與光} from <Picture 4>.
<Picture 1> ([Shot 1] first frame): fully_preserved - the video starts exactly from its framing, poses and lighting.

detailed_description:
{風格句} Shot on a {mm} lens at f/{…}, {看得見的效果}. Everything moves at real-time speed.
[Shot 1] The shot begins from <Picture 1>: a {景別詞} of {誰}, at {高度} from {水平角度}. The frame shows {畫面邊界}. The camera holds this framing as a static shot until the final frame. {第 0 幀：每人一句，位置、手上、視線} {觸發} {主動作, and in that same instant 後果} {每人反應一句} From about the {T2}-second mark to the end, {停住句}. At the end, {收尾狀態}. {人數句}, and {嘴閉句}.

overall_soundscape:
{環境音一句}. {動作聲＋時間點}. {之後剩下的聲音}. These are the only sounds in the video.

non_diegetic_music:
N/A
```

**B. 對白鏡：參考模式＋首幀＋角色裁圖＋音色檔**

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is {角色寫法}, {年齡}, on the character sheet <Picture 2>, with {4–6 個看得見的外觀錨點}. {穿戴態宣告}
<Subject 2> is {場景} on the scene sheet <Picture 3>, with {地標、具名光源}.
<Picture 1> is the first frame of [Shot 1], showing {首幀構圖}.
<Audio 1> is the voice-timbre reference for <Subject 1> (S1).

summary:
[keyframe completion + reference generation + audio reference] A {秒}-second vertical 9:16 {景別詞} of <Subject 1> in <Subject 2> that begins from <Picture 1>. {一句：誰對誰說話、看得見的目的}. The shot ends with {收尾狀態}.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - {臉、髮、服裝} from <Picture 2>{; 穿戴態 is retained for the whole video}.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): {fully_preserved／partially_preserved} - {地標與光} from <Picture 3>.
<Picture 1> ([Shot 1] first frame): fully_preserved - the video starts exactly from its framing, poses and lighting.
<Audio 1>: reference - the speaking voice of <Subject 1> follows the timbre of <Audio 1>; the original recording and its words are not reused.

detailed_description:
{風格句} Shot on a {mm} lens at f/{…}, {看得見的效果}.
[Shot 1] The shot begins from <Picture 1>: a {景別詞} of <Subject 1>, at {高度} from {機位}. The frame shows {畫面邊界}. {His whole head stays inside the picture for the whole video.} {站位、朝向（中近景以內只寫朝向）} {His eyes are on 場內目標或畫面方向, and they stay there for the whole video.} His voice: {聲音鎖}. Right at the start, <Subject 1> (S1) says it {語氣、語速}, in the timbre referenced from <Audio 1>: <d>[Chinese] {台詞}</d> Exactly as the voice stops, the lips close and the jaw ceases all speaking motion. This single line is the only speech in the video. {反應一句} The camera holds this framing as a static shot until the final frame. {收尾狀態句}

overall_soundscape:
{環境音一句}

non_diegetic_music:
N/A
```

**C. 沒有首幀**：照 A 或 B，拿掉首幀那幾處（`<Picture 1>` 的定義句和 retention 行、`The shot begins from <Picture 1>`、summary 的 `keyframe completion`），參考圖從 `<Picture 1>` 起重新編號；起手狀態照 `查表-H3句型.md` §7 寫。summary 和 [Shot 1] 一樣先寫景別詞：`[Shot 1] A {景別詞} of {誰}, at {高度} from {方位}.`

**D. 加尾幀**：A 或 B 再加三處——`subject_definitions` 加 `<Picture N> is the last frame of [Shot 1], showing {結尾構圖}.`；`retention_analysis` 加 `<Picture N> ([Shot 1] last frame): fully_preserved - the shot ends exactly on its framing.`；描述最後寫 `the shot ends on <Picture N>`，和尾幀的景別、位置、視線一致。

## 3. 景別詞（圖和影片共用；鏡頭表的中文六選一逐字對應）

| 景別 | 景別詞 | 畫面邊界句 | 另外要做 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 遠景 | an extreme wide shot of {場景}, with {誰} standing small at {地標} | The frame shows the whole {場景}: {地標}. {誰} is small in the frame, her whole body visible from head to feet. | 列地標；室內加室內錨句 |
| 全景 | a full shot of {誰} | The frame shows her whole body from the top of her head to her feet, with a little of the floor below her feet. | 全身穿著；地標 |
| 中景 | a medium shot of {誰} | The frame shows her from the top of her head to her waist. | 不寫腰以下 |
| 中近景 | a medium close-up of {誰} | The frame shows her from the top of her head to the middle of her chest. | 場景寫成模糊背景 |
| 近景 | a close-up of the face of {誰} | The frame shows her from the top of her head down to her collarbones, and her face fills most of the frame. | 同上 |
| 特寫 | an extreme close-up of the face of {誰} | Her face fills the whole frame: the top edge of the frame crosses her hairline and the bottom edge crosses her chin, so the top of her head, her neck and her collar are outside the frame. | 角色參考圖用 `{ID}_face` |

- 近景＝close-up，特寫＝extreme close-up；特寫寫成 close-up 會出近景。
- 中近景以內，場景定義句寫 `<Subject N> is {場景} on the scene sheet <Picture N>, seen here only as a softly blurred background with {光}.`，retention 寫 `partially_preserved - only the softly blurred background and {光} are retained.`；角色句只寫看得見的穿著。
- 室內錨句只用在全景、遠景，放在畫面邊界句後面；中近景以內不寫站在哪件家具前、地標清單。
